Which cleaning service should I choose?

Routine cleaning is a starting point for everyday upkeep. Deep cleaning focuses on a more thorough reset, while move-in and move-out cleaning is designed around a change of home. If you’re unsure, choose “Help me choose” in the quote form.

Can I ask about specific rooms or tasks?

Yes. Use the “Tell us about your space” field to describe your priorities, the rooms you’d like help with, and any tasks you want to discuss. The scope can be confirmed before scheduling.

What information should I include for a quote?

Your general location, approximate home size, preferred timing, and cleaning priorities are a helpful start. You don’t need to include your full street address at this stage.

Does requesting a quote book a cleaning?

No. A quote request starts a conversation. The scope, price, availability, and appointment time need to be confirmed before a cleaning is booked.
